Scroll to travel through every version of pandymic.com, rebuilt from what the Internet Archive kept: the hand-made tables, the Elvish dictionary, the wallpaper generator, a blog, a wiki, a few too many "Hello world!" pages, and the URLs that turned into emoji.

Prequel · 2001 – 2002Before pandymic.com

⚔ Side quest

Geography 12, on CD-ROM

Before pandymic.com there was a CD. Geography 12 notes (the lithosphere: plate tectonics, volcanoes, weathering, glaciers) as a website that ran from the disc: autorun started a bundled Apache and Perl on localhost:43667, pages were stitched together with server-side includes, and a Perl script dealt out a random quiz from a bank of 66 questions. The quiz runs again, in JavaScript.

v0 · January 2003Temporarily down

2003-01-22

The first thing the Archive ever saw

The oldest capture of pandymic.com is a holding page: the PANDYMiC Media Network is "currently unavailable", offline "to perform a complete redesign". The redesign was three weeks away.

v1 · February 2003 – February 2005The grey shadow-table

2003-02-11

A 610-pixel table, hand-shaded

The first real design: a white page floating on grey, with a drop shadow built from GIF slices. The logo was four separate images (PANDYMiC) and the menu ran Index, Features, Media, Links. The home page said, in its entirety, Welcome to the Main Page!

2003-04

TEMP: INDEX.ABOUT.HTML

Text sub-menus arrived: About, Contact, Articles, Columns. Behind almost every one of them was the same letter-spaced placeholder naming its own file. Most of them would say exactly that until 2005.

2003-06

A résumé, as a single GIF

The exception: the résumé page, typeset as one image. (The contact page got the same treatment; that GIF is lost.)

⚔ Side quest unlocked

Necrowombicon on PACult

The Penny Arcade Cult was home from 2001 to 2007, and Necrowombicon was its yearly meet-up. Its page on pacult.com counted down to NWC 2003 with a clock sirblack drew: Countdown to NWC (thank you sirblack). Here it counts up instead, from the minute it ran out (as archived). The 2001 report, NWC 2002 and the 2003 plan survive too. Gabe announced it on Penny Arcade ("This year we have POWER!", "our third Wombicon"), linking here.

⚔ Side quest unlocked

Necrowombicon 2003

The portal at /nwc/ had the same clock, bigger and darker. Its own pieces were never archived; they are rebuilt here from the lighter PACult copy, recoloured to the palette of the one tile that survived. After the weekend, the countdown gave way to its last tile: Oh snap, it's over! Here it reads Time since, counting up from 25 July 2003, 22:22 UTC (as archived).

⚔ Side quest unlocked

FF2000

"The unofficial home of Penny Arcade's FF2000", a Flash splash and a link to Penny Arcade. "This site doesn't currently exist. It'll come up eventually." Flash is gone too; the movie file survives in the archive.

2003-10

The big reshuffle, and 黒

One-piece logo. Features split into Articles, Columns, Reviews and Misc; Media into Artwork, Audio and Video; Index gained Résumé, Portfolio and a Cam. A new button appeared: Tools. And behind the home page, a faint brush-drawn 黒 (kuro, "black", as in sirblack), its top stroke breaking up into the header. The watermark image itself was lost; this one is redrawn from that surviving stroke.

2003-12

Anime, and friends in the sidebar

An Anime button, leading to PANDiME on its own subdomain. The sidebar's 88×31 buttons now included DingoStick and Aaron Ballman's Ramblings, next to MegaTokyo, Penny Arcade and the NFCR. /pacamp moved out to pacamp.pandymic.com.

⚔ Side quest unlocked

PANDiME

An anime fan site with its own sticky-note-and-cat layout. Two announcements survive ("Initial Site Layout Up...", "Meow...", then "Sakuracon 2004!" and "Busy as hell."). The layout's images do not.

2003-12-09

sirblack's English/Elvish dictionary

The first tool, and the one that outlived everything: type an English word, press go, get Sindarin and Quenya from Olórin Mithel's word list. It moved through three layouts and two hosts and was the most-requested page on the site. It is running again in this rebuild, with all 4,646 entries, recovered from a 2009 database dump.

Background art: two elven archers by Justin Thavirat (1996), © Blizzard Entertainment, washed out behind the text as on the original page.

2004-01-18

Wallpaper Generator v1.0

"Thrown together in about 4 hours": pick a design, a background colour, your resolution and your name, and a PHP script crops and stamps a JPEG just for you, numbered. Renders #711 to #714 went out in August 2004, from one shared counter. The art layers were never archived; they were recovered from the archived renders, and the generator works again.

⚔ Side quest unlocked

PAC Amp

A Penny Arcade Winamp skin by Swishiee (PAC_Amp.wsz, 96 kB), followed by the PACult buddy icons and, that December, the PACult Secret Santa sign-up page. In 2005 swish.pandymic.com served the same page. The skin itself survived the archive intact: try PAC Amp in Webamp.

2004-05 → 09

The Japanese title

For a summer the header switched to a Japanese variant. Both of its images 404'd by the time the crawler came back in 2011, so this is one piece of the site nobody can see again.

2004

PandaCAM

A 320×240 webcam, served by a twenty-line PHP script called cam.pgi. The archive caught exactly one frame.

2004-12

Broken!

By December the generator's Finish button had been replaced with a red Broken!. Two months later, the whole site went with it.

v2 · 2005 – spring 2006Wipeout, and a long shadow

2005-02-11

Wipeout...

The entire home page. XHTML 1.0 Strict, though.

2005-08

The long shadow

A new mark: PANDYMiC with its orange dot, the "i" casting one long shadow off into the distance. Nothing else.

2005-09

The Lexicon survives the wipe

The dictionary came back as a standalone box, now in both directions: English → Elvish and Elvish → English.

2005-10

A receipt

This receipt is somehow related to approximately $40 worth of Alcohol. I'd appreciate if someone could please tell me exactly what I had that night. Thanks! The receipt survived in a 2014 server backup: Pronto, Kyoto, 11 April 2005, 21:41. A beer, a vodka tonic, an orange juice, a mentaiko-and-squid pizza and two Canadian Club Black Labels: ¥3,320. Mystery solved, twenty-one years late.

2005-12

A Japanese dictionary on the front page

The splash grew a search box: English ⇄ Japanese, with match modes up to "Full Regexp". Underneath, a preview of what was coming: Home - Wiki - Blog - Elves.

v3 · June 2006 – 2010The blue sakura

2006-05-21

"Server crash"

The oldest surviving blog post, on a stock WordPress (Kubrick, the default theme), is about the server crashing. A fitting start.

2006-06

"ws": a window onto a sakura tree

A full redesign: a gradient night sky, a cherry tree on a black hill, a reflection under the frame and a mascot peeking in from the left. Tabs across the top: Home, Blog, Gallery, Japanese, Elvish, Wiki.

2006-12

Contact Me, in the sky

The empty sky got a contact form: From, Subject, Auth, and a Message of at most 255 characters.

2006

jdic

The Japanese dictionary from the 2005 splash got its own red room in the new layout.

2006 → 2008

Gallery2

Photo albums on Gallery2, the open-source PHP gallery of the day, embedded in the sakura layout. The archive kept the album pages but hardly any photos; they're back, from the original files in a 2014 server backup: PAX 2005 and 2006, and Japan 2005 (Kyoto, Tokyo, Osaka, Hiroshima). In 2008 the gallery moved from /gallery2/ to /g/. The albums are private: viewing them needs an unlock link.

2006 → 2009

PANDiKI

A MediaWiki whose main page reads, in full, This magical wiki! Its skin was never archived.

2008-01

"Now with more XHTML 1.1"

"Server wipe, v.2. Reinstalled Everything!" (Jan 3). A post written in the Flock browser (Jan 16). Then the page validates as XHTML 1.1: "Opera, IE, Safari, Firefox and even Lynx all render the page very neatly."

⚔ Side quest unlocked

PANDYMiC Lite, at BCIT

Meanwhile, at school: a course blog on BCIT's student server, built on Blogger's classic templates and published by FTP, with a PHP script to list the posts. Speech-bubble menus, quizzes that highlight the right answer on hover, "The A to Z of Programmer Predilections", and a COMP 1850 site called "Wonderful world of Mike".

2008

280,671 searches

The Lexicon's final form: radio buttons for direction, and a counter. On 30 August 2008 it read 280,671 phrases searched by 21,431 users. It was the most-requested page on the domain.

2008-11

Tables out, divs in

The same design was rebuilt as an XHTML 1.1 div layout using stylesheet_xhtml.css. The Internet Archive never captured that file; Common Crawl did, in July 2010, so the new markup is styled again here. The fuller table version stays the main v3 page.

2009-07

"Dear Visitor"

For a while the home page became a letter to prospective clients: Development, Optimization, Design, Security. "I don't build websites, I build web applications." The Internet Archive never saw it; Common Crawl did. Its stylesheet and icons weren't crawled.

2010-09-08

Last light

The sakura layout's last capture. By now the Lexicon's counters printed blank.

2010 – 2013Static

2010 → 2013

No signal

Three years without a single capture of a page. In October 2011 the crawler came back for the old images and got 404s, which is why some 2003 chrome had to be rebuilt.

⚔ Side quest unlocked

Dori & Mike

The quiet years had a good reason: a wedding website. Drupal 6, a full-screen backdrop, an ornate frame and a strip of engagement photos by Stacey Malleck. Behind the scenes it kept the guest list and printed every RSVP card and envelope as a PDF. The archive never saw it; this is rebuilt from the server backup, without the guest list.

⚔ Side quest unlocked

eightyfour

Meanwhile: eightyfour, a web design & development studio, and its 2013 splash page: a "Generation 3.0 Web knowledge meter" wiring every skill into a brain, under a ticker of "two web developers' experience". Rebuilt from the Photoshop file, the ticker counts live, adding up both developers' years since 2002 and 2008. The public site itself was one line of HTML: Eightyfour.ca.

v4 · 2013 – 2014Drupal sandbox

2013-06

www., officially

The bare domain starts redirecting to www.pandymic.com, and in December a Drupal 7 site appears: Bartik theme, a login box and one node. This domain is presently functioning as a sandbox while I re-develop the site. Behind it, from the site's own 2014 database: four "assets" for hotlinking (New desk, Out of this World, Lego shovel, "Link quest, Zelda boobs?"), each served through a PHP module that logged every view to Google Analytics with GeoIP. Its index page said only Hello World!

2014-12

...and back again

Eighteen months later the redirect flips: www. now points at the bare domain, and it stays that way.
