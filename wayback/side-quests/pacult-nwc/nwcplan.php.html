Day One, Friday July 25th:
  • Where: Lester's Arcade in Burnaby, B.C.
  • When: Starting around 4PM, ending around 11PM.
  • What: Gaming and socializing. There will be live DJs all night long and you may indulge in plenty of arcade gaming, pool tables and pay-for-play LAN action. A band Called The Tenant will hit the stage around 7PM and play for about an hour. So around 8PM, Tycho and Gabe will hit the stage themselves for a little Q&A. Once they've had enough of us badgering them with our fanboy questions, another band called Darkest of the Hillside Thickets will play for another hour or so. The whole thing should wrap up at about 11PM. We all need our rest for the Saturday game fest.
  • Cost: $15 at the door. Games are separate, although some will be on freeplay in the arcade.
  • Other Info: Everyone there will be Penny Arcade fans, so don't be afraid to talk to people!
Day Two, Saturday July 26th:
  • Where: The Tiffany Ballroom in the Hilton Hotel in Burnaby, B.C. (6083 McKay Ave. Burnaby BC, v5h 2w7 to be exact. Phone #: 604-438-1200)
  • When: 8AM Saturday morning to 4AM Sunday morning (longer if needed, but Fatrick needs sleep).
  • What: All day BYOC (Bring Your Own Computer) LAN, console, and table top gaming (table top schedule).
    • Game Tournaments (prizes yet to be decided):
        • PC
      • UT2K3 (we'll use the demo so everyone can play): Start time: 10AM. Bombing Run, 6 on 6 teams, best of 3 matches, 10 minute time limit.
      • Deathmatch (open entry): Start time: high noon! Best of 3 seperate maps, 10 minute time limit each match.
      • Starseige Tribes: Start time: 2PM. Capture the flag, default ruleset, 6 on 6 teams, best of 3 maps, 15 min. time limit on each map.
      • Soldier of Fortune 2: Start time: 4PM. Infiltration CTF, best of 3 maps.
      • Tribes 2: Start time: 6PM. Capture the flag, default base ruleset, 6 on 6 teams, best of 3 maps, 15 min. time limit on each map.
      • Battlefield 1942: Start time: 8PM. Conquest, 6 on 6 teams, best of 3 maps, default ticket scores per map.
      • Rise of Nations: Start time: 9PM, play till its done! Free for all, default ruleset, up to 8 players (I think it can handle 8 players).
      • Starcraft: Start time: 5PM, play till its done! Free for all, default ruleset, up to 8 players.
      • Warcraft III: Start time: 7PM, play till its done! Free for all, default ruleset, up to 8 players.
      • Armagetron: Start time: 10PM. Free for all, as many people as we can get, best of 20 min. match.

        Console
      • Halo: Start time: 11AM (probably last the longest of all). 4 on 4 teams, best of 3 matches, capture the flag, team king of the hill, and team deathmatch, choice of map.
      • Super Smash Brothers Melee: Start time: 9AM. Standard 4 player elimination.
      • Soul Calibur: Start time: 1PM. Elimination.
      • Soul Calibur 2: Star time: 3PM. Elimination.

  • Cost: $25, plus an extra $5 if you bring a computer.
  • Other Info: There is underground parking ($4 for the whole day, purchase tickets at the front desk as con attendees are considered guests) and a loading dock for people bringing computers. Everyone who said yes to bringing their own computer when they registered will be receiving an email in regards to exactly what they need to bring for the LAN. If you don't receive an email about this, please let us know.
Day Three, Sunday July 27th:
  • Where: The largest of the 3 rooms of the Tiffany Ballroom in the Hilton Hotel in Burnaby, B.C. (6083 McKay Ave. Burnaby BC, v5h 2w7 to be exact. Phone #: 604-438-1200)
  • When: 9AM to 5PM (everyone out, Fatrick has to sleep).
  • What: Hang out with whoever is left. There will be a premature celebration of Sim's 19th birthday (actual date: July 28th). There should still be plenty of console and table top gaming to be had. For those of you interested, there will be a big Warhammer 40K game on this day.
  • Cost: Free if you have your nametag from Friday or Saturday, otherwise $5.
  • Other Info: Seriously, you don't have to go home, but you have to leave at 5PM.
