HS Fucked This Up
  • Necrowombicon Report

    Necrowombicon kicked major amounts of ass! Every bit of it was loads of fun. The whole thing wasn't so much a convention as a get together. It was kinda like a meeting of friends or something, at least that's what it felt like for me.

    I flew in to Seattle on Friday and had a hell of a time first getting near the Space Needle and then finding the Center House. But I eventually made it there. Once I was inside the food court type place, I easily found everybody. I saw two groups all clustered around tables. There was a group of people staring at Tycho and a group of people staring at Gabe. Since Tycho was closest when I walked in, I sat down on the fringe of his group and listened in. Everyone was bunched in a circle around Tycho and he was, as expected, entertaining us all. When things got slow, we all prodded him with sticks until he said something funny. After 10 or 20 minutes, things slowed down enough that Tycho was able to get up and meet some of the newcomers. I introduced myself as Sim and shook his hand. He seemed pretty surprised to see me there, or to finally see me in the flesh, or something. I talked with him for a little bit and then headed towards Gabe. The first person I recognized was Porkfry, who recognized me and waved me in to a seat. Again I listened in for awhile until there was a long enough pause that I could introduce myself to Gabe and we talked about the cult for a bit.

    There was about 30 or 40 people there on Friday night, I guess. Everyone was just hanging out, talking about games, Penny Arcade, and other nerdy things like that. I met Bizket and Kara, but that was about it. Things started to dwindle around 8, so that's when I took off.

    I showed up at the Seattle Science Center around 11 the next day, for the Toy Test thing. If you didn't know, they were showing off some unreleased as of yet games for Gamecube and Playstation 2. There was a pretty long line to get in to the playtesting part of the Science Center, but Tycho spotted me and let me cut in with his posse, which included Brenna, Gabe, Kara, Safety Monkey, and some other guy Tycho used to be in a band with. Sorry, I didn't catch his name. Brenna was very nice, I really enjoyed meeting her. Tycho was very cool as well, he really made me feel welcome. Safety Monkey was tall. That's all I have to say about him. Anyway, the people there choose which game you get to play for 20 minutes. I ended up getting to play and review Tony Hawke 3 for the PS1, which turned out to be just like Tony Hawke 1 and 2. No one else really seemed to be impressed with any of the games. There were a couple games Tycho and Gabe wanted to play, but it wasn't worth the wait, even if they picked the game you wanted to play. Safety Monkey was the only one who really had fun because he found out you can knock over the opponent in whatever NBA Live game he got to play for the PS2. He has many fond NBA Jam memories.

    After that we grabbed something to eat and then went up the Space Needle. I had a great time just following them around. I was a fanboy in heaven. I mostly talked with Tycho and Brenna, who were extremely nice to me. While we were up in the Space Needle, Brenna took a picture of us all. I think it's funny that she'll have a picture of all her friends, plus this weird teenager she met for a couple hours who worships her husband. Also, I'm sure the photo will look stupid because I ended up standing at the end of the line next to Safety Monkey who is way taller than me.

    After we came down from the Space Needle, Tycho said he would give me a ride to Gameworks, but his car was full of Monkey. So I walked around the Seattle Center, checking things out. I made my way over to Gameworks at about 6. Everyone had to get out on the sidewalk while Bizket yelled instructions. When we started filing in, Porkfry got everyone yelling crazy things and I saw lots of people giving us, a crowd of nerds, funny looks. It was cool though because Penny Arcade pretty much owned Gameworks for the next 4 hours. The announcers there even pimped Penny Arcade a couple times.

    The arcade kicked ass. There were a ton of great games to play, but I mostly stayed on the racers like Daytona USA 2, this Indy racer, and Star Wars Pod Racers. I played most of the weird games at least once, like the fire fighter, the hang glider, the water skiing one.

    I was so caught up in the excitement that I totally forgot to take any pictures up until 9:30 or so on Saturday night. So I took a bunch of pics in about a 10 minute time frame right before I left. Porkfry was just like I imagined him, a very cool guy with lots of energy. I met stk (the only PAC member besides Porkfry who recognized me), which was cool. After all that fun, I went up to Canada, but that's not worth talking about, especially after Necrowombicon. Seriously though, all of Canada I saw was the water off the ferry and the very center of Victoria.
