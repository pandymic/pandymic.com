HS Fucked This Up
Necrowombicon 2002 will be held in Vancouver B.C. Canada on the weekend of the 16th, 17th, and 18th of August, 2002. (Needless to say, any reference to cost is in Canadian funds) It is mostly being organized by fatrick, so if you have any questions that are not answered here or in the forum, please email him at the official Necrowombicon email: thenecrowombicon@hotmail.com. If you would like to register for the con (which is encouraged), all you have to do is email: thenecrowombicon@hotmail.com with your real name, online nickname, and which days you plan to attend. The current list of registered members can be found here.

All the news, information, and feedback can be found in the Necrowombicon 2 forum.

    Here is a list of plans for the con and details about them (cost, time, etc.), in the words of fatrick with some editing by me (Sim):

    August 16th: Friday Night, First Night of Con:
    ***This is not official, yet!***

    Everyone can meet at Playdium, which is much bigger, more group oriented arcade like Gameworks in Seattle (site of the first con). The Person I have spoken to there has given me the following information:

    Everyone who is with Necrowombicon can purchase playcards with 4 hours of gaming for $20, which is a mighty good deal. To make sure some hosers don't get in on our deal, be sure to bring a Gamer Corps Card. Just print one of those babies out, fill it in, and flash it when you line up. If you just can't manage to print one of those out, you can probably get the discount by saying you're with the Necrowombicon or you're staying at the Holiday Inn. That should cover it.

    We have about 150 cards put aside, as I don't think we'll be needing more than that. We're going to start around 7PM and stop around 11PM. They have a lounge there if people wish to order food and drinks, and generally socialize.

    He has also offered us the use of an IMAX screening room for 1 hour. (he thought Penny-arcade was a cartoon and not a comic) I can see no real reason why we would need it, but any ideas would be much appreciated.

    The other thing about this is: they have the right to close down sections of the facility for groups. Now... I nor any of you would probably not like to see the fighting game section closed for some group while we're there... SO to counter this, we need to book ahead and reserve, to do this I have to book the amount of people (my guess is anywhere between 80-150 people) and I have to give a deposit of $5 per person. Seeing how I'm getting the deposit back, that money is my concern.

    Playdium is Located in a Giant Mall called Metrotown Center. In it there are Shops of every kind; Electronics Boutique, Games Workshop, Tons of Foreign Game stores for all your Import Game needs, food courts, Chapters, you name it... it's there. There's also a Multiplex Theater if we should choose to see one of the latest hit (re: Crap) movies out there. Down the street (quite a ways) is a store called Sakura Media, if you want ANYTHING anime, it's there. Including most Video Game soundtracks, and J-rock/J-pop CD's. Got my sexy 'Cowboy Bebop' soundtrack from this place: http://www.sakuramedia.com/.

    August 17th, All Saturday, Second Day of Con:
    ***This is official!***

    We have a Banquet Room booked at the Holiday Inn in Metrotown. We have the room from 9-am to 12:30-am. There will be a LAN, so bring your computers and network gear. Admission will cost $15. You must pay me in cash (as I don't accept credit, debit or check) and I will give you a nametag, with your name on it. Those nametags will be the sign that you have paid, and will give you free roaming access.

    There will be many open tables, so bring your D&D, Warhammer Armies, etc. (don't forget the Mountain Dew) We have 2 TV's rented for the whole day, Bring your PS2, X-box and Gamecube games, as I will have the consoles there. Someone please bring a PS2 controller hub, thanks. If you bring any equipment (computers, netgear, games) I cannot afford to take responsibility for it! If any gear is stolen, that is NOT my responsibilty and I will NOT be held liable. Bring gear at your own risk. To put you at ease, there is only one door in, and one door out, so if people were going to nab your PC tower, it would be pretty obvious. I'm sure everything will go well with that so do not worry.

    Unfortunately both the bands scheduled to play have canceled, so there will be no live music. For the reasons why, please read this topic on the board. There will be a mic and podium for announcements but that is all.

    We have a cash bar from 7 - 12 (since you cannot bring your own booze). You will be checked for ID at the bar; legal age in B.C. is 19. Minors are still allowed on the premises when the drinking commences.

    There will be 4 gaming slots for people who want to organize tabletop games
    Slot 1 from 9am - 12pm
    Slot 2 from 12pm - 4pm
    Slot 3 from 4pm - 8pm
    Slot 4 from 8pm - 12am

    If you would like to run a game during any of the alloted times, then let me know (thenecrowombicon@hotmail.com). Tell me your name, what slot you would like to run your game in, what game you're going to play, and how many people you want in your game (i.e. Bob Smith, Slot 1, running D&D 3rd edition, 6 players). I will post a list of the games that are being organized, if you want to register for a game let me know the same way: name, slot, game, game organizer (i.e. Jane Doe, Slot 1, playing D&D 3rd Edition with Bob Smith). I will put a list of the people that have registered for the games up along side the games being organized.

    If you want to organize a video game tournament, please email me in the same way you would to register a tabletop gaming slot.

    August 18th and on, Sunday Through Whenever, Rest of Con:
    ***Other events, not part of con.***

  • Fragmart: August 22nd-25th. 600+ LAN party in Vancouver. More info. can be found at Fragmart.com. (thanks Goalcam)
  • Moby Concert: August 18th. Tickets available at Ticketmaster. (thanks Bethor99)
  • Possible overnight party for adults (19+) the weekend before the con, with bands and a beer garden. More info. to come. Also there is a yearly fireworks display in Vancouver called "The Symphony of Light". All the info. about that can be found here.

    Guest Appearances:

    Gabe and Tycho will be there! Do you need anything more? Batjew lives 15 min. away from the con, so if he doesn't show, then the lynchings will proceed. Staff from Electronic Arts, Relic Entertainment and Radical Entertainment will be there, wether as Official spokespeople or not is yet to be determined, but they will be hanging out with us none the less. I might be able to convince Walter '|2|' Costinak (designer of the PA logo) to rear his head, but he's a lazy old goat who never leaves the house, so no guarantees.

    Accomidations:
    ***This is official.***

    Holiday Inn Metrotown
    4405 Central Boulevard
    Burnaby B.C.
    V5H 4M3

    Call for reservations at: 604-438-1881 (local) or 1-877-323-1177 (toll free). You need a credit card to make a reservation. It is too late to sign up for the special group rate, so you are on your own now. In case you haven't figured it out, this is the hotel where day two of the con will be taking place, so it's just convenient if you can get a room here. You're welcome to stay wherever you want.
