PAC Secret Santa!

Alright folks. The Sign-up period has passed. Thank you for everyone who has decided to participate.

Before we continue, I'd just like to set up some rules. Firstly, please don't give your giftee your identity. I'd like for everyone to enjoy this little event. The idea is for everyone to be surprised. Secondly, gifts should be from the heart, but put effort into them. Items can be put together as well as purchased. Let's set a nice round value for gifts around $5 - $10. I won't limit how much you can spend but I understand that not everyone here is financially endowned, so please don't go off and buy someone a $100 CD Player and get upset when YOUR Santa gives you Macaroni and glitter Christmas card. Thirdly, don't go off and give away peoples addresses without their prior consent. Not everyone enjoys being added to a mailing list and getting spam in the mail as much as you do.

Most importantly, please remember to have FUN! This is what this event is all about.

Merry Christmas!
