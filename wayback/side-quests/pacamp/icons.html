Buddy Icons for AIM by Swishiee

 

 

 

How to install:

Just click on one of the images, and AIM will ask you if you'd like to make it your buddy icon. Just click ok, and you're done. Also, you can save the image onto your hard drive and then press F3 and configure you buddy icon settings.

If you don't have AIM (AOL Instant Messenger), you can get it here.
