Volcanic Features

Volcanism: The transfer of lava from one location to another.

 

Lava

Lava is simple molten rock from the earth's mantle that has found a way to the surface. Sub-surface lava is called magma

When there is only a small opening in the curst, a volcano is formed. Lava and other material form a cone shaped structure surrounding the opening.

Crater: Funnel shaped opening at top of cone

Caldera: Great basin formed when a portion of the crater or vent blasts off

 

Intrusive Volcanic Activity

  1. Dyke: Magma that cools in a vertical crack.
  2. Sill: Magma that cools between strata forming a sheet
  3. Laccolith: a sill with a large enough volume to raise the overlaying strata.
  4. Batholith: Huge mass of magma that has intruded into a wide area with no known depth.
