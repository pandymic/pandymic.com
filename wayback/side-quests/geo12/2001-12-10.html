Wind Erosion

The predominant surface in deserts is actually just bare rocks, such as sand has blows away.

Reg: Rocky Surface

Only 20% of deserts have accumulations of wind blown sand called Erg. This sand is blown around and carried all over the world.

Dunes: Depositional features of wind blown sand. They are dynamic features under constant change.

There are 3 Variables in Dune formation:

  1. Sand Supply
  2. Wind Velocity
  3. Vegetation
