PANDYMiC.COM Wallpaper Generator v1.0

First and foremost, thank you for visiting my page. I went through quite a bit of an adventure putting this script together, so I decided that my efforts were well worth sharing with the rest of the internet.

This script was thrown together in about 4 hours and as of Jan. 18, 2004, it can still be considered an 'initial' release. Anyone who knows me well enough will know how lazy I am; and, though, I promise that I will eventually add more wallpapers for you to choose from, I can't guarantee it will be any time in the near future (or on a regular basis, for that matter)

Alright, well here's the concept. You select a wallpaper from the list and you select your resolution. If you wish, you can also customize some minor details on the wallpaper itself. I'll eventually (read: not any time soon) throw up an FAQ if anyone so much desires. Until then, feel free to fire me off an e-mail.

I take no credit for any of the artwork that is presented and featured in these wallpapers, All this script does, is puts them together and makes it all look fancy. Thank you to 4chan.net for constantly providing me with a mountain of resources and references. You guys rock.

Enjoy! ^_^

2. Resolution (Size)

Please Enter your desisired image size.
The format is:
  [width]x[height]
The minimum values are 640x480 and the maximum values are 2048x1536
The 'x' must be lowercase.
Incorrect values will default to 1024x768

3. Background Color

Please Enter the background color.
The format is:
  [rr][gg][bb]
Only 6 digit hex values are accepted.
Do not add the leading '#' symbol
Incorrect values will default to 000000 (black)

4. Your Name (optional)

Please Enter your name.
The format is:
  [your name]
Up to 16 characters are allowed
Anything more than 16 characters will be truncated.
This field is optional

5. Generate

Press the button to generate your wallpaper
Please do not press the button more than once.
The Process may take several seconds.
