Join the mouse swarm?

See the mouse pointers of other visitors move around in real time, and let them see yours. A few friendly bots wander around too, so you're rarely alone; their cursors are tagged "bot", and a short note appears when anyone comes or goes.

What's shared

  • Your pointer's position, as a fraction of your window, and the page you're on. They go to my server and are passed on live to everyone else in the swarm.
  • A random ID for this visit. No name, no account, no cookies.

What isn't

  • Nothing is stored. Positions exist only while you're connected and vanish when you leave or close the tab.
  • Clicks, keystrokes, anything you type, and touch input are never sent.

As with any visit, my server and Cloudflare see your IP address; the swarm uses it only to limit abuse. Your choice is remembered on this device (no cookie) until you switch the swarm off. Privacy policy
